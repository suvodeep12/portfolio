Rift Herald started as a small tool for a friend's Discord server.

## What it does

It tracks League of Legends ranked progress and announces daily LP changes through Discord commands and scheduled updates.

## Engineering notes

The project combines the Riot Games API, Discord interactions, scheduled work, SQLite, and account tracking by PUUID rather than only by display name.
